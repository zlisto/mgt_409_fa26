AI vibe coding

  • Describe what you want in words; AI writes the code
  • You need to understand the structure and flow, i.e. the "vibe"
  • Key skill - writing the prompt

AI agents

  • Put the model in a loop: plan → call a tool → read the result → repeat
  • Tools: web search, read/write files, run Python, call APIs
  • Example: find new sales prospects, design a new product line, clean up my inbox, trade stocks until I'm rich, prove the Riemann hypothesis, create a drug to cure cancer, etc.

Images and video

  • Vision = AI can see and undertand images
  • Products, people, websites, videos
  • Build vision based tools for your agents

Apps

  • Wrap your agent in software people can actually use
  • Customer support chatbots, sales outreach tools, operations dashboard
  • Web apps or mobile apps

MCP servers

  • A standard plug-in layer so agents can call different applications via chat
  • Gmail, Stripe, Slack, Spotify, Canvas — or build your own
  • MCP servers are products for AI agents — not for humans.

Tokenomics

  • Tokens (words) → dollars: what each model call actually costs and why
  • Design cost-effective agents — cheap model vs. smart model
  • Guardrails and shorter prompts save money as well as risk

Attention Weights

  • An attention head computes a weight between each pair of words
  • The weights tell you how much a word pays “attention” to another word

Context Dependent Embeddings

Consider these sentences that use the word bat

  • A bat flew out of the cave
  • The bat pooped on the ground
  • A bat bit me in the neck
  • I’m afraid of a bat because it is like a rat with wings
  • A bat flew out of the baseball player’s hand
  • I hit a home run with the metal bat
  • No one swung a bat harder than Babe Ruth.

Transformers Talking

  • A generative transformer turns the word embedding vector into a probability distribution over all words in the vocabulary
  • Text is generated by sampling from this distribution
  • These types of transformers are also called large language models (LLMs)

AI-Maxxing Frame

  • Once you embrace AI and decide to AI-maxx, you're in a new frame — you stop bolting AI onto the old workflow
  • Not “use AI to fill in a spreadsheet” → no spreadsheets; AI updates your records database directly
  • Not “use AI to skim resumes in HR” → AI interviews every candidate in depth and maps real strengths
  • Not “use AI to draft customer-support replies” → AI owns the resolution loop and only escalates true edge cases

New Tech, New Paradigm

  • Every transformative technology starts by bolting onto the old world — until someone sees what the tool is really for
  • Early cameras just filmed stage plays — then someone invented cinema
  • AI today is that kind of leap: design the next paradigm, not a faster version of yesterday
