Scenario and data

Scenario

Campus Customs sells Yale merch. Your job is to build an agent that helps with two growth questions: (1) is someone in a photo wearing a Campus Customs product (so they might be worth outreach), and (2) would a Campus Customs ad video land with a given customer profile — for example a Yale student or a parent.

Use your PORTKEY_API_KEY for any AI model calls in the agent. Use OpenAI models through Portkey: gpt-5.6-luna, gpt-5.6-terra, gpt-5.6-sol, or gpt-6-astra (your choice). You may want a smarter model for harder vision or agent steps. Yale’s course budget assumes luna for the full semester.

Work one problem at a time with your vibe coder. Type each problem in your own words. Do not paste the page URL, and do not screenshot the problems. Submit your work as hw3.zip (the last problem shows which files to include).

Data

Download data.zip. Unzip it so you have:

  • data/products/ — many Campus Customs product photos (the catalogue)
  • data/test_images/ — four influencer-style photos; each filename tells you whether a Campus Customs product is in the shot (true or false)
  • data/videos/ — a Campus Customs ad video (ad_humble.mp4)

This homework uses Campus Customs / Yale Bulldog Blue as a local setting for class. It is not a partnership with the shop.
