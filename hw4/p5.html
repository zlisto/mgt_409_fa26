Homework 4: College Street Music Hall Guest Site

Scenario and data

Scenario

College Street Music Hall is a live-music venue at 238 College Street in New Haven (collegestreetmusichall.com). Guests want a small site they can log into, look at upcoming shows, and ask a customer-service bot about bags, tickets, door times, and what is on the page in front of them.

Your job is to ship a FastAPI backend and a React (Vite) frontend: categorize the FAQ (no vector store), look up the right section with an LLM, support hashed-password login and new accounts, save each user’s chats in the database, brand the pages like the venue, and let guests talk to the bot by text, by a screenshot of the current browser view, and by voice. The running app should use a local SQLite file (csmh.db), not JSON files. Use the OpenAI API. Work one problem at a time with your vibe coder. Type each problem in your own words. do not paste this whole page. Zip your work as hw4.zip.

This is a class project. The venue did not hire the class. Do not email, call, or otherwise contact the venue or hotel partners.

Before you start, make folders backend/ (Python) and frontend/ (Vite). Add one piece per problem as you go. The zip layout is the last problem.

Data

Download hw4_csmh.zip. Copy csmh.db into backend/. JSON in the pack is the same seed content for inspection. Demo usernames and passwords are in the pack README. Graders score grounding against this pack, not the live calendar.

Tables in csmh.db:

  • users — username, display_name, password_salt, password_hash (no plaintext passwords)
  • faq — policy entries; category_id starts empty (Problem 2)
  • categories — empty until you write rows
  • shows — sample calendar
  • tickets — fictional confirmations linked to a username
  • conversations / messages — empty; you store each user’s chats here
  • meta — venue name, address, password hashing settings
