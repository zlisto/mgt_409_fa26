Homework 1: AI Financial Document Analysis

Scenario and data

Scenario

Spoke & Wrench Bicycle Repair is a small New Haven shop. The owner keeps messy records: receipts, bank and credit card statements, a lease, emails, and handwritten notes.

Your job is to use AI to process these documents into a January income statement. Use the OpenAI API for this task. Work one problem at a time with your vibe coder (do not ask it to finish the whole assignment in one prompt). Type each problem in your own words. do not paste this whole page. Zip your work as hw1.zip.

Before you start, make a folder with prompts/ and output/. Add one script per problem as you go. The zip layout is the last problem.
