# College Street Music Hall — course FAQ (paraphrased)

This is a **homework snapshot**, not the live venue FAQ. Do not email or call the venue for this assignment.

Public site: https://collegestreetmusichall.com/faq/

## Location (`faq-location`)

College Street Music Hall is at 238 College Street, New Haven, CT 06510.

## Buying tickets (`faq-tickets-buy`)

Advance tickets are sold through the venue website. The public FAQ listed Friday box office hours as suspended and the Redscroll Records box office as not currently available. This pack has no live box-office hours.

## Refunds (`faq-refunds`)

Sales are final. No refunds or exchanges unless a show is canceled or postponed. Openers can change.

## Resale (`faq-resale-tixel`)

The public FAQ names Tixel as the official fan-to-fan exchange. Some shows may not be eligible. This pack does not resell tickets.

## Age (`faq-age`)

Unless a specific event says otherwise, shows are all ages.

## ID and alcohol (`faq-id-alcohol`)

ID is for drinking, not for entering an all-ages show. Guests 21+ who want alcohol need a valid, non-expired, government-issued photo ID. Without it, they are treated as under 21. Violations can mean ejection.

## Seating (`faq-seating`)

Configuration changes by show. Loge and balcony (when used) are seated. Some floors are seated; others are GA standing. Many GA shows have limited back-of-room chairs, first-come, not guaranteed.

## Accessibility (`faq-ada`)

Accessible, but layout depends on the show. Typical GA floor: ADA seating on a house-left rear platform. Seated shows: ADA in Gold Circle and Section 109. Loge and balcony are stairs-only; the public FAQ states there is **no elevator**.

## Bags (`faq-bags`)

Bags, backpacks, and containers larger than **12 × 6 × 12 inches** are not allowed. This pack does not mention lockers.

## Prohibited items (`faq-prohibited`)

Examples: weapons, illegal drugs, outside food and drinks (including empty refillable bottles), laser pointers, professional photo/recording gear, drones, selfie sticks, skateboards/rollerblades/bicycles, and animals except as allowed under ADA or state/local law.

## Re-entry (`faq-reentry`)

Public Know Before You Go notes: no re-entry. Smoking areas are described as available.

## Will call (`faq-willcall`)

Pickup in the purchaser’s name with photo ID. Public notes say the box opens about two hours before door time.

## Food (`faq-food`)

Geronimo Tequila Bar & Southwest Grill is described as serving tacos in the lobby before and during shows. Bars also sell snacks such as chips.

## Drinks (`faq-drinks`)

Multiple full bars: drafts, cans, liquor, wine, soft drinks, water. Alcohol still follows ID rules.

## Coat check (`faq-coat-check`)

Seasonal, first-come, first-served, can fill up.

## Parking (`faq-parking`)

Discounted Temple Street Garage parking via ParkWhiz is mentioned (about two blocks). Crown Street Garage is often noted nearby. Other lots and street parking exist. This pack does not sell parking.

## Hotels (`faq-hotels`)

Public FAQ names New Haven Hotel and Hotel Marcel as partners. This homework must not book rooms or call hotels.

## Photo / recording (`faq-photo`)

Per show, at the artist’s discretion. Do not promise phones or cameras are always allowed.

## COVID (`faq-covid`)

Snapshot: no venue-wide proof-of-vaccine or negative-test rule, but policy can change per artist or government. Point guests to the event page.

## Contact (`faq-contact`)

Public FAQ lists 203-867-2000 and info@collegestreetmusichall.com. **Your agent must not send email or place calls.**
