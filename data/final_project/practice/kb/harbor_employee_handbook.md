# Harbor Collective — Employee Handbook (Practice Excerpt)

**Company:** Harbor Collective (practice fixture for MGT 409 Final Project)  
**Effective:** January 1, 2026  
**Audience:** All full-time and part-time employees

## 1. Return policy (customer-facing staff)

Harbor Collective accepts returns of unused products in original packaging within **30 days** of purchase with a receipt or order confirmation. Final-sale items marked as such at checkout are not returnable. Store credit may be offered at manager discretion when a receipt is missing.

## 2. Shipping SLA (standard domestic)

Standard domestic orders ship within **2 business days** of payment confirmation. Express shipping ships within **1 business day**. These SLAs exclude weekends, company holidays, and weather delays. International shipping timelines are not covered in this excerpt.

## 3. PTO accrual (full-time)

Full-time employees accrue **15 days** of paid time off per calendar year, prorated in the first year of employment. PTO requests of 3 or more consecutive days require manager approval at least **10 business days** in advance. Unused PTO does not roll over except as required by local law.

## 4. Data handling

Employee Social Security numbers and customer payment card numbers must not be pasted into AI tools or shared in Slack. Use the approved redaction workflow before escalating tickets that contain PII.

## 5. What this handbook does **not** cover

This practice excerpt does **not** define a remote-work stipend, parental leave duration, or equity vesting schedule. Questions about those topics must be escalated to People Ops—do not invent a policy.
