Homework 2: FiftyFlowers Outreach Agent

Scenario and data

Scenario

FiftyFlowers sells bulk and wholesale flowers for weddings, events, and professional florists (fiftyflowers.com). They want more business customers—wedding planners, event designers, floral studios, and venues that buy in volume—not one-off retail shoppers.

Your job is to find target businesses on the live web, crawl their pages for contact info, score how well they fit the ICP, draft outreach emails, and build a report page. Do not send the emails. Prefer published business contact pages. Do not harvest personal emails from social media. Use the OpenAI API for this task. Work one problem at a time with your vibe coder (do not ask it to finish the whole assignment in one prompt). Type each problem in your own words. do not paste this whole page. Zip your work as hw2.zip.

Before you start, make a folder with prompts/ and output/. Add one script per problem as you go. The zip layout is the last problem.
